meet your artist

ANNABELLA
TESSIER

Annabella works primarily in black and gray illustrative tattooing, with a focus on composition, texture, soft detail, and designs that feel specific to the person wearing them.

The Scarlet Dagger was built as a private studio instead of a traditional open-floor shop. That means appointments are one-on-one and the environment can stay calmer, quieter, and more personal.
