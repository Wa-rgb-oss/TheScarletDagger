Before You Book

Policies that keep
appointments running smoothly.

Deposit+

A $50 non-refundable deposit is required to secure an approved appointment.

Rescheduling & Cancellation+

At least 48 hours notice is required to move a deposit to a rescheduled appointment. With less notice, a new deposit may be required.

Deposit Expiration+

Deposits expire 90 days after payment if an appointment has not been completed or appropriately rescheduled under studio policy.

Design & Placement+

Requests may be adjusted for size, placement, longevity, or style fit. Submitting a request does not guarantee acceptance or an appointment date.
