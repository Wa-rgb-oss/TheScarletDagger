A few house rules.

01

$50 deposit. Non-refundable and required to secure an approved appointment.

02

48 hours. Give at least 48 hours notice to move the deposit to a rescheduled appointment.

03

90 days. Deposits expire after 90 days under the studio’s booking terms.

04

A request is not a booking. Annabella reviews each idea for style, placement, size, and availability before offering a date.
