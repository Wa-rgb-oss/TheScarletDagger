BOOKING POLICIES // READ FIRST

BEFORE
you book.

A few things to know before requesting an appointment. These policies help keep scheduling clear and make sure everyone knows what to expect before a date is confirmed.

01

THE $50 DEPOSIT

A $50 non-refundable deposit is required to secure an approved appointment. The deposit reserves time in the schedule and is handled according to the studio's booking terms.

02

48-HOUR NOTICE

At least 48 hours notice is required to move your existing deposit to a rescheduled appointment. Cancellations or rescheduling with less than 48 hours notice may require a new deposit.

03

90-DAY EXPIRATION

Deposits expire 90 days after payment if the appointment has not been completed or appropriately rescheduled within the studio's policy.

04

REQUESTS ARE NOT BOOKINGS

Submitting a tattoo request does not automatically confirm an appointment or a specific date. Annabella reviews each idea for style, placement, size, and availability before moving forward.

05

DESIGN + PLACEMENT

Designs may need to change in size, placement, complexity, or composition so the finished tattoo works on the body and ages appropriately. Annabella may decline projects that fall outside her style or scope.

06

ARRIVAL + APPOINTMENT

Please arrive prepared for the agreed appointment time. Significant lateness can affect the amount of work that can be completed and may require rescheduling depending on the day.
